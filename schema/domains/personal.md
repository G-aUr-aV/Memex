# Personal domain

- **Health** (`areas/Health.md` hub, with sub-pages like `Health - Vitamin D.md`): transcribe lab values exactly (value, unit, reference range, date, lab) into a table on the marker's page. Never round or convert units silently. Visits get the reason, what the clinician said, medication changes and follow-ups. **Never diagnose or recommend treatment**; write `## Questions for my doctor` instead. Any interpretation gets `confidence: low`. No insurance member IDs or government IDs; point to the document in `raw/` instead.
- **Finance** (`areas/Finance.md`): tracking only, never investment or tax advice. Subscriptions get `amount, currency, cycle, renewal_date, payment_hint` (last 4 digits only) and a cancel link. Keep an index of warranties, insurance and document locations. No account, card or ID numbers.
- **People** (`people/`): relationship, how you know them, `birthday`, `last_contact`, `cadence_days`, gift ideas, things they care about. Record only what the owner stated explicitly. Keep it kind and factual.
- **Projects** (`projects/`) cover trips, purchases, events and home projects. Structure: goal, options and criteria (a comparison table for purchases), decision and why, logistics, lessons. For trips: itinerary, bookings as pointers (no booking codes or passport numbers), packing, lessons for next time.
- **Goals** (`goals/`): quarterly goals with key results scored 0.0-1.0 weekly during `/weekly`.
- **Ideas** (`ideas/`): `status: seed|exploring|active|parked|done`. Link each idea to goals and concepts; promote it to a project when it becomes active.
- **Reflections** (`reflections/`): weekly and monthly syntheses built from journal entries. Quote the owner's words with links to the dated daily notes. Describe patterns and ask questions; never psychoanalyze or give therapy-style advice.
